DIRECTIONS

Site & District Administrators

If you are a Site Administrator or District Administrator in PowerSchool Professional Learning and serving as a supervisor of Principals and Assistant Principals participating in APLDS…

  1. 1

    Log into PowerSchool PL.

  2. 2

    Click the Administration menu.

  3. 3

    Click Ad-Hoc Reporting.

  4. 4

    Click the blue New Report button for a Transcript Ad-Hoc Report.

  5. 5

    In the New Report window, click the Select All check box twice to remove all check marks.

  6. 6

    Now check the following boxes:

    • a

      Course Number

    • b

      Course Title

    • c

      Section End Date (Click the "Equals" drop box and select On or After then type 06/01/2026 in the box.)

    • d

      APLDS Clock Hours – Units (Click the "Equals" drop box and select Greater Than or Equal then type a 1 in the box.)

    • e

      *Last Name

    • f

      *First Name

    • g

      ALSDE ID

    • h

      Sites—For Sites, you will want to select just your LEA. To do so, click the plus sign next to the All My Sites check box and drill down to your LEA.

  7. 7

    Now, click the Run button to run the report. It may take the program some time to process the data. You will receive an email when the report is done. Click the link in that email to open the report.

  8. 8

    You can download a CSV file of the report which can be pulled into Excel or Google Sheets by clicking the Download CSV link in the upper right corner of the Report screen.
