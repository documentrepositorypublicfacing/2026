Governing Board & Director

Standard I: Board Composition

A. The composition of each governing board shall comply with the following schedule of membership categories and specific numerical representations:

B. Governing boards whose service area is comprised of more than 12 school districts may have representation from classroom teachers and administrators from each of the districts in the service area. The number of teachers or administrators shall be increased proportionally (two teachers for each administrator) to ensure that all school districts are represented. Board members shall serve staggered terms of three years and shall be eligible for one reappointment term.

Required Documentation
  • A list of board members by name, membership category, and term of office submitted to ALSDE.
  • Sign-in sheets from the three governing board meetings submitted to ALSDE.
Standard II: Board Appointment and Meetings

A. Initial appointments to board membership and/or reappointments to board membership shall be made by official resolution(s) of incumbent governing boards in duly constituted meetings. Nominations for board membership shall be solicited equitably from local education associations, local superintendents, In-Service Center Directors, and the State Superintendent of Education.

B. Each governing board shall conduct a minimum of three (3) meetings per calendar year.

Required Documentation
  • All correspondence related to nominations for board membership submitted to ALSDE.
  • Designated meeting dates submitted to ALSDE 30 days prior to actual meeting.
Standard III: Board Member Orientation and Professional Learning REVISED

A. All new board members shall participate in orientation/training to become acquainted with the ARIC Accountability Standards as well as the specific goals, projects, and programming of the relevant ARIC.

Required Documentation
  • Documentation of board member orientation participation submitted to ALSDE. Documentation of board member orientation participation (sign-in sheet or submission of orientation training materials) submitted to ALSDE.
Standard IV: Bylaws

A. Each governing board shall develop and adopt official bylaws to which its internal structure and operation shall conform. Adopted bylaws must address election of board officers; term of officers; quorum for official business; committee structures and functions, if any; parliamentary procedures; and other issues and concerns as determined by incumbent boards. As specified in each governing board's bylaws, voting on decisions that must be made prior to the next governing board meeting may be conducted electronically.

Required Documentation
  • Minutes from governing board meetings submitted to ALSDE.
Standard V: Board Responsibilities

A. Commensurate with statutory authority, each governing board shall fulfill the following responsibilities:

  1. In cooperation with the ALSDE and host institution, determine the policies for the operation of the center, including days and hours of operation;
  2. Approve programs and activities that address needs as identified by needs assessments and/or student achievement data, and the plan to access the center's programs and services by its constituent schools and districts;
  3. Review and acknowledge the in-service center's annual budget;
  4. Delegate to the in-service center director the authority and responsibility for the daily operation of the center, including the development and maintenance of necessary records (financial and programmatic) and the coordination of programs and activities; and
  5. Review the center's official report of the programs and trainings offered.
Required Documentation
  • Results from the needs assessment from all LEAs submitted annually to ALSDE.
  • Documentation of alignment between programs/trainings and needs assessment results submitted annually to ALSDE.
Standard VI: Board Minutes

A. Each in-service center must maintain accurate minutes of all board meetings and distribute minutes to board members in a timely fashion.

Required Documentation
  • Minutes from the three governing board meetings submitted to ALSDE.
Standard VII: Director

A. Each regional in-service center shall have a full-time director. Current part-time directors shall be grandfathered in. The governing board chairperson and/or his/her designee must serve on the search committee and take part in the interview process as a voting member.

Required Documentation

None specified.

Standard VIII: Director Orientation and Professional Learning REVISED

A. All new in-service center directors shall receive orientation from a committee of current in-service center directors. All new in-service center directors shall receive orientation from a committee of current in-service center directors, assigned on a rotating basis so the responsibility does not fall consistently on the same directors.

B. All in-service center directors shall participate both individually and collectively in continuous improvement through professional learning and growth endeavors.

Required Documentation
  • Documentation supporting the director's participation in continuous professional learning submitted annually to ALSDE.
Standard IX: Position Description

A. A position description for the center director shall be developed and approved by the governing board and the institution's appropriate official.

B. The in-service center director is an ex-officio member of the Governing Board.

C. The in-service center director shall serve as Executive Secretary for the governing board and shall perform administrative duties as determined by the board within the framework of personnel policies of the employing institution.

D. Directors are compensated in accordance with the established salary matrix.

Required Documentation
  • A copy of the approved position description submitted to ALSDE.
Standard X: Annual Report REVISED

A. By December 1 of each year, each governing board shall approve and submit to the State Superintendent of Education and/or his/her designee an annual report of programs and activities including at least the following components:

  1. Impact of the ARIC's programming on improving the skills of teachers and leaders across all professional learning initiatives provided; Evidence of the ARIC's impact on the growth and development of teachers and leaders — specifically their knowledge, skills, and practice — through targeted, high-quality professional learning that addresses regional needs, as demonstrated by the number and types of professional learning opportunities offered, participation rates, and participant satisfaction;
  2. Quantitative and qualitative data for the number of programs, participants, and results of evaluations; and
  3. Compliance with Accountability Standards as approved by the State Board of Education.
Required Documentation
  • Annual reports submitted to ALSDE by December 1.
  • Report contains number of participants and evaluation results.
  • A reflective narrative describing improvement efforts submitted annually to ALSDE.

Programs

Standard I: Assessment

A. Each in-service center shall review the performance of its service area schools in mathematics and reading/ELA on state assessments and utilize that information as a part of its pre-programming assessment.

B. In-service centers shall utilize additional data to determine the professional learning needs of the districts and schools in the service area as they relate to the improvement of student learning. The determination may include, but not be limited to: educator effectiveness data, formal needs assessment instruments, dialogue with districts and schools, summative student assessment data, feedback/observations from regional staff, and specific district/school requests.

C. Results from the professional learning needs assessment will be provided to each LEA.

Required Documentation
  • Results from the needs assessment from all LEAs submitted annually to ALSDE.
  • Documentation supporting the use of multiple data sources submitted to ALSDE.
  • Documentation of alignment between programs/trainings and needs assessment results submitted annually to ALSDE.
Standard II: Programs REVISED

A. Professional learning must address the needs identified as outlined in Standard I: Assessment.

B. The selection of professional learning opportunities will be guided by evidence-based practices and professional learning standards, consistent with applicable state legislation including ARI and OMI. The selection of professional learning opportunities will be guided by evidence-based practices and professional learning standards, consistent with applicable state legislation.

C. Centers shall maintain documentation demonstrating alignment between needs assessment results and program offerings.

Required Documentation
  • Documentation of alignment between program offerings and needs assessment results (e.g., topic distribution charts, session alignment spreadsheets) submitted annually to ALSDE.
Standard III: Communication

A. Information about the programs and services of the in-service center will be made available to the center's participating districts and will be posted electronically for open access. All trainings offered during the summer months will be posted digitally by April 15.

Required Documentation
  • Electronic posting of summer trainings completed by April 15 each year.
Standard IV: ALSDE/Center Collaboration

A. The State Superintendent, or his/her designee, will appoint a member of the ALSDE to serve as a liaison who will provide the following coordination and support:

  • Each in-service center director will ensure that ALSDE appointees have information regarding upcoming meetings as quickly as possible.
  • The State Superintendent or his/her designee and in-service center directors will strive to meet annually to discuss on-going work, possible additional collaborations, programmatic updates, and other relevant information.
Required Documentation
  • All collaboration efforts documented and submitted to ALSDE.

Finance

Standard I: Budget REVISED

A. Each in-service center director shall submit to the ALSDE a prepared budget for the fiscal year. The governing board and the institution acting as fiscal agent prior to submission to the ALSDE for final approval by the Deputy State Superintendent of Education for Finance must approve the budget. Each in-service center director shall submit to the ALSDE a prepared budget for the fiscal year. The governing board and the institution acting as fiscal agent must review and acknowledge the budget prior to submission to the ALSDE for final approval by the Deputy State Superintendent of Education for Finance.

B. The budget shall include designated expenditure categories and amounts in compliance with the following schedule of line items:

  • Salaries & Benefits – includes actual salaries paid from the funds (indicate in FTE) and benefits provided.
  • Travel – in-state and out-of-state, with rates, purpose, and travelers indicated.
  • Consultants (contracted & staff) – purpose of work and approximate contract amount.
  • Materials/Supplies – office supplies, books, postage, etc. Any single item purchase of $500 or more must be inventoried.
  • Equipment – any purchase of $5,000 or more must be inventoried.
  • Research/Evaluation – funds for research conducted for the benefit of ALSDE.
  • Indirect Cost – capped at 10%.

C. Consultant honoraria amounts shall reflect the most recent board-approved rates. If the expertise or unique ability of a consultant requires a higher honorarium, the director must obtain governing board approval. Consultant honoraria amounts shall reflect the statewide rate of $400 per full day and $200 per half day. If the expertise or unique ability of a consultant requires a higher honorarium, or if the honorarium exceeds $5,000, the director must obtain governing board approval.

Required Documentation
  • Expenditures submitted to ALSDE organized by designated categories.
  • Indirect cost calculations submitted with the budget to ALSDE.
Standard II: State Financial Accountability

A. The governing board must approve each instance in which center funds are utilized to finance the cost of client participation in out-of-state professional learning programs/conferences. In-service center funds shall not be authorized for use for schools or school systems other than those for whom state funds were appropriated.

Required Documentation
  • Governing board approval documented and submitted to ALSDE.
Standard III: Financial Reports REVISED

A. Quarterly Expenditure Reports are to be submitted to the ALSDE by the 30th day after the end of the quarter.

B. The End of Year Quarterly Report is to be submitted to the ALSDE by November 15.

C. Adjustments between expenditure line items are allowed providing the cumulative adjustment does not exceed 10% of the total amount budgeted unless it is paid for through university sources, as confirmed permissible by the ALSDE Finance Division. Adjustments between expenditure line items are allowed providing the cumulative adjustment does not exceed 10% of the total amount budgeted unless it is paid for through university sources.

Required Documentation

None specified.

Standard IV: Approving Authority

A. The State Superintendent of Education is the final approving authority for the center's budget.

Required Documentation

None specified.

Standard V: Funds Management

A. All funds allotted to the in-service centers will be receipted to a separate fund number and disbursed in accordance with fiscal policies approved by the chief financial officer of the university and the ALSDE.

B. A consultant whose salary is paid by in-service center funds may not receive an honorarium from center funds for conducting business for that center. This restriction does not prohibit an employee of one center from receiving a standard honorarium for providing consultant services for another center; however, the center employee must not be paid by the home center and the center being provided consultant services during the same period. Leave time must be requested while being paid for services to another center.

C. Payment of an honorarium to a program consultant is authorized and permissible only if services are rendered. In-service centers shall not use funds, directly or indirectly, to finance course credit, including the payment of stipends to be used for credit courses and/or tuition of any kind.

D. Director salaries are determined based on the teacher pay scale with established percentage increases, as set forth by the ALSDE.

E. Travel will be paid in accordance with state and institutional policy.

F. Persons designated to perform administrative duties resulting from institutional or agency involvement in official in-service center consortia shall not receive compensation from center funds beyond the regular annual contract salary paid by the institution or agency.

Required Documentation

None specified.

Research and Evaluation

Standard I: Research

A. As mandated in the original enabling legislation and as approved by the governing board, each in-service center shall serve as a data-gathering center for research and/or research collaborator in areas of public education.

Required Documentation
  • Research/evaluation documentation submitted by December 1 to ALSDE.
Standard II: Evaluation

A. All in-service centers shall use a common evaluation process to gather evaluations of in-service center programs by program participants. Acceptable evaluation processes include common survey platforms (e.g., PowerSchool survey) or other instruments that satisfy this standard as clarified by the ALSDE. ARIC Needs Assessments may fulfill this standard where appropriate.

B. The State Department of Education will conduct an internal evaluation of the ARICs based solely on each center's compliance with the standards, to be conducted at a minimum of every five years.

C. An exit conference will be held upon completion of evaluation.

D. The final evaluation report will be shared with the director at least two weeks before results are shared publicly.

E. A corrective action plan for non-compliance will be developed and submitted to the ALSDE liaison within sixty days after the final evaluation report is received.

F. A corrective action plan review may be conducted six months after implementation.

G. Additional reviews may be conducted based on continued non-compliance.

H. Should the Alabama State Department of Education or Alabama State Board of Education determine an external evaluation of ARIC is necessary, such an evaluation will be based solely on the center's compliance with the standards herein specified and shall be conducted at a minimum of every five years. Funding for such evaluation to be paid by ALSDE as funds allow.

Required Documentation

None specified.
