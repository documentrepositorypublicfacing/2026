Annual Evaluation Reset Requests (TE and LEAD)

Alabama State Department of Education — Teaching Effectiveness (ATGP & LEAD)

Permalink: https://bit.ly/TE-reset

Evaluatees can now request their annual evaluation reset through the Teaching Effectiveness application. Reset requests must be approved by the Evaluatee’s Evaluator/Reviewer. Once a reset is approved, all current annual evaluation data will be permanently lost — make copies of any documents or data before proceeding.

Evaluatee — Requesting a Reset

Reasons

Valid Reasons for an Annual Evaluation Reset

  • The Evaluatee changed Systems or Schools after starting their annual evaluation.
  • The Evaluatee needs to change their Continuum after starting their annual evaluation.
  • The Evaluatee changed positions after starting their annual evaluation.
  • The Evaluatee realized the information entered in their annual evaluation is inaccurate.
Step 1

Navigate to Your Dashboard

  • Teachers — Click the ATGP – Teacher Evaluation System tile on the Teaching Effectiveness dashboard.
  • Central Office Evaluatees (LEAD) — Click the LEAD AL – District Administrator Evaluation System tile on the Teaching Effectiveness dashboard.
Step 2

Submit a Reset Request

  1. 1 From your dashboard, click the Request Annual Evaluation Reset button.
  2. 2 On the Request Reset screen, enter a reason for the reset request.
  3. 3 Click Submit to send the request. To cancel without submitting, click Cancel to return to the Teaching Effectiveness dashboard.
Data Loss Warning: Once a reset is approved, all data associated with the current annual evaluation will be permanently lost. It is recommended that users save copies of all documents and data before requesting a reset.

After submission, an e-mail notification is sent to the Evaluator/Reviewer. The Evaluatee also receives a copy for their records.

Step 3

Check or Cancel Your Reset Request

After submitting, two additional buttons appear on your dashboard:

  • View Reset Request Status — Shows the current status of the reset request.
  • Cancel Annual Evaluation Reset Request — Cancels the pending request. A reason for cancellation is required.
Approved

After the Reset Is Approved

Once approved, the Evaluatee receives an e-mail notification. The Evaluator/Reviewer is copied on the notification.

On the next login to Teaching Effectiveness, the Evaluatee can begin a new annual evaluation. User settings must be reselected on the Settings page before starting the new annual evaluation.

Evaluator / Reviewer — Managing Reset Requests

Step 1

Access Reset Requests

From the APLS dashboard, click the Manage Reset Request tile. This opens the Manage Reset Requests screen, which lists all pending reset requests.

Reviewers can see reset requests for all Evaluatees across their entire district, not just their site.
Step 2

Approve or Reject a Request

  1. 1 On the Manage Reset Requests screen, click the Approve or Reject link next to the Evaluatee’s name.
  2. 2 On the Approve Reset screen, optionally enter comments. Comments are not required for approval.
  3. 3 Click Approve (or Reject) to finalize. The Evaluatee will receive an e-mail notification; the Evaluator/Reviewer will be copied.
