Obsidian + Claude: How They Fit

The System

Obsidian — the knowledge layer

  • Local Markdown files on your computer
  • Organized vault of notes, summaries, references
  • Nothing goes to the cloud unless you choose

Claude — the AI layer

  • Connected directly to your vault via the desktop app
  • Reads your notes and context files — no pasting required
  • Outputs land back in the vault, ready for next time

06

Data Privacy by Design

FERPA compliance isn't a disclaimer at the end. It's a design principle from the start.

5 MIN

What Goes In — What Never Does

Data Privacy
✓
Claude can see this
→ Meeting notes & agendas
→ Publicly available information
→ Your own professional documents
→ Formatting & document creation tasks
→ Research & reference work
FERPA Line
✕
Never goes to Claude
→ Student names, IDs, grades, or records
→ Staff evaluations or personnel files
→ Anything under FERPA, HIPAA, or a confidentiality obligation
→ Internal legal or HR communications

The workflow is designed around this line — not as an afterthought, but as the first decision.

Note: Because the vault is what Claude has access to, keeping data out of Claude means keeping it out of the vault. Options include mounting only a subfolder or maintaining a separate vault for protected data — ask your AI platform what works best for your situation.

Here's a workflow I saw demonstrated today: Obsidian (free, local note-taking app) serves as a personal knowledge vault — meeting notes, email summaries, role context, and reference documents. An AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini) with access to that vault drafts documents, builds public-facing resources, and carries context forward across sessions — no more starting from zero each time. Outputs are built as single HTML files, published to GitHub Pages, and shared via short links — no web team, no CMS, no cost. As the vault grows, the workflow grows with it. The documents you can build and publish will change as your knowledge base does — what starts as a simple reference page can evolve into something more connected and complex over time. The full presentation is at drjmc.link/AIplaybook if you'd like a frame of reference for what was demonstrated — what this looks like in practice may be different from what you'd expect or recommend.
