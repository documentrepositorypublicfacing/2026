Overview

ABOUT

What This Process Is

Professional study providers — organizations, agencies, individuals, LEAs, or other entities — use the Alabama Council for Leadership Development's (ACLD) Instructional Leadership Professional Study Application (ILPSA) portal to submit a professional study for review. If approved, the study earns Professional Learning Unit (PLU) status, which counts toward maintaining Instructional Leader Certification.

Note: The ACLD and ALSDE do not endorse the groups, organizations, agencies, individuals, or vendors that submit applications. ACLD approval covers only the content and delivery of the professional study itself.

Submit at least two months before the study's planned implementation date — this gives the ACLD adequate time to review and gives providers time to respond to any ACLD recommendations. The ACLD does not review applications for retroactive approval.

Portal: leadershipalsde.egrantsmanagement.com

Step-by-Step Submission

STEPS

Getting an Account & Submitting an Application

  1. 1

    Request ILPSA portal access

    You must have an account in the portal before you can submit anything. On the portal home page, find the “Professional Study Providers Request Application Access” box and click Click Here.

  2. 2

    Complete the access request form and send it

    Fill in your name, address, job title, email, phone, and website, then click Send.

    If requesting access for an LEA: include the provider's mailing address, plus the superintendent's name, phone number, and email address — the superintendent will be shown as a contact on any professional study application submitted through the account.
  3. 3

    Set a password and log in

    Once ACLD creates your account, you'll receive an automated email from eGrants Management with a link to set your password. Use it to log into the portal.

  4. 4

    Open the application page

    After logging in, your LEA/Agency/Company name appears at the top of the screen with a View Application link. Click it to go to the application page.

  5. 5

    Check application status

    The Applications page shows every application your LEA/Agency/Company has, in one of three statuses:

    • Approved — reviewed and approved by the ACLD Committee; ready to enter in PowerSchool Professional Learning.
    • Active — submitted, but not yet reviewed.
    • Expired — approved previously but past the three-year deadline; must be resubmitted for review.
  6. 6

    Start a new application

    Select the appropriate fiscal year and standard, then click Start New Application at the bottom of the applications page.
