External Credit Requests

PowerSchool Professional Learning

https://bit.ly/PSPLexternal

Eligibility caveat: External Credit Requests are approved only for educators whose certificate has lapsed and who are recertifying before December 31, 2026. They will not count toward any certificate renewal, or toward any recertification after December 31, 2026.
PART 1

To Enter External Credit Requests

  1. 1

    Click the Professional Learning menu.

  2. 2

    Click the My Professional Learning button and select My Transfer Credit Requests.

  3. 3

    On the My Transfer Credit Requests screen, click the blue Create External Credit Request button.

  4. 4

    On the form that appears, enter the following:

    • a

      Training Name — must be very descriptive. Generic titles such as "Faculty Meeting" and "Data Meeting" are not sufficient and will be denied.

    • b

      Start Date

    • c

      End Date

    • d

      Hours — this number must match the number of hours indicated on the attached Certificate of Completion.

    • e

      Description — be as descriptive as possible.

    • f

      Documentation — use the File Attachment feature. Documentation must be a certificate of completion clearly showing the number of hours completed.

  5. 5

    When the form is complete, click the blue Submit for Credit Approval button in the bottom right corner.

PART 2

To Review and Approve / Deny External Credit Requests Site or District Admin

  1. 1

    Click the External Credit Request menu.

  2. 2

    On the Transfer Credit Approvals screen, you will see three tabs: In Progress, Submitted, and Archived. You should be under the In Progress tab where all pending requests are found. To review one, click the name of the Training.

  3. 3

    That request will open. Review it, check the documentation, click the Clock Hours check box, and confirm the requested hours.

  4. 4

    Click the blue Approve button.

    If denying: If the request does not provide enough information or adequate documentation, click the red Deny button and provide an explanation of what is missing.
