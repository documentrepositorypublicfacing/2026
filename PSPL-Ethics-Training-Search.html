Find Courses That Award Ethics Clock Hours

  1. 1

    Log into PowerSchool Professional Learning.

  2. 2

    Click the Courses menu and select the Advanced Search link under the Search box.

  3. 3

    On the Course Catalog screen, open the Credit Types by clicking the Credits handle, then check the box for Ethics Clock Hours, and then click the Apply Filters button.

The resulting screen will display all currently available courses that will award Ethics Clock Hour credit.
