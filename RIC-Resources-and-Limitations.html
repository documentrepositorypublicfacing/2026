This guide summarizes available space, parking, and scheduling considerations at each Regional Inservice Center, for ALSDE divisions, sections, or programs planning training sessions or events. Use the search box to filter, or contact a director directly using the email links below.

General Best Practices — All Regions

1Advance Notice
  • Request space as early as possible — minimum 2–4 weeks, one month preferred
2Provide Details
  • Accurate participant count
  • Date and time preferences
  • Special equipment or setup needs
  • Technology requirements
3Communication
  • Work directly with the RIC director
  • Add the director and/or designee as an instructor in PSPL
  • Confirm parking arrangements and communicate to participants
4Flexibility
  • Be aware of academic calendar constraints
  • Consider alternate dates/times if first choice is unavailable
  • Have backup plans for larger groups
5Cost Considerations
  • Most RIC spaces are free
  • Large-group spaces (75+) typically incur fees
  • Off-campus venues may be necessary for very large groups
