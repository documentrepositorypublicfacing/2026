Reviewing Transcripts for APLDS Clock Hour Credit

School Principal Leadership and Mentoring Act — 2026-27 Directions

https://bit.ly/APLDStranscript

PART 1

Principals & Assistant Principals

  1. 1

    Log into PowerSchool PL.

  2. 2

    On the Professional Learning menu, select the following in order:

    • A

      Click the Transcript menu.

    • B

      On the My Transcript page, in the Filter Credits section, change Completion Date to End Date.

    • C

      Add June 1, 2026 in Select A Date.

    • D

      Choose APLDS Clock Hours in the Credit Area filter.

    • E

      Click the blue Update button.

  3. 3

    The program will filter out all other credit types and just display your APLDS Clock Hour credit for the 2026-27 school year with a total listed at the top of the transcript.

PART 2

Site & District Administrators (Supervisors)

  1. 1

    Log into PowerSchool PL.

  2. 2

    Click the Administration menu.

  3. 3

    Click User Accounts.

  4. 4

    Search for a person using the search box and click the blue Search button.

  5. 5

    When their name appears on the screen, click the drop arrow next to their name and choose View Transcript.

  6. 6

    On the My Transcript page, select the following filters in the Filter Credits section then click the blue Update button:

    • a

      Change Completion Date to End Date.

    • b

      Add June 1, 2026 in Select A Date.

    • c

      Choose APLDS Clock Hours in the Credit Area filter.

  7. 7

    The program will filter out all other credit types and just display your APLDS Clock Hour credit for the 2026-27 school year with a total listed at the top of the transcript.
